CEG Converge 2026 Recap

Rekindling Bonds, Celebrating Innovation, and Charting the Next Era

CEG Converge 2026: Rekindling Bonds, Celebrating Innovation, and Charting the Next Era

Published by CEGAANA • Computer History Museum, Mountain View, CA • September 2026

On Saturday, September 26, 2026, the historic halls of the Computer History Museum in Mountain View, California reverberated with the unmistakable spirit, pride, and energy of the College of Engineering, Guindy (CEG).

Alumni spanning six decades—from pioneering leaders of the 1970s to recent graduates and student scholars—convened for CEG Converge 2026. From our executive morning Leaders Forum to afternoon keynotes, venture panels, and reunion celebrations, the gathering proved once again that our alumni community remains an unmatched powerhouse of innovation and camaraderie.

Honoring Our Roots: The Founders of CEGAANA

Every vibrant global movement begins with visionary individuals willing to invest their time and heart. At Converge 2026, our entire community paused to extend our deepest gratitude to the Founders and Builders of CEGAANA who had the vision and foresight to create and lay the strong foundations on which we continue to build and grow.

Many thanks to Raj Nathan, Kribakaran Narasimhan, Ravi Raju, T. Sridhar, Karthik Siva, Sesh Bala, Janaki Akella, and all previous board members and volunteers for their foundational contributions to CEGAANA. Their tireless commitment laid the bedrock for every scholarship funded, every betterment project executed, and every conference hosted today. We salute their foresight and legacy!

Invaluable Support: Our Generous Sponsors

Executing a flagship conference in Silicon Valley requires significant resources. We extend our warmest appreciation to our corporate sponsors and generous alumni donors. Their early financial commitments lifted budget anxieties, ensuring our committee could focus wholeheartedly on delivering a memorable attendee experience.

We are thrilled to report that our sponsors reported exceptional engagement throughout the day. Please take a moment to explore the tabletop trifolds, scan the sponsor QR codes, and actively patronize and partner with the enterprises that stood behind CEG.

Key Highlights of the Conference

  • The Leaders Forum: An exclusive VIP morning roundtable connecting founders, CXOs, and angel investors to shape high-impact initiatives for the diaspora.
  • Keynote by Radha Ramaswami Basu (CEO, iMerit): An inspiring keynote exploring ethical AI, purposeful scaling, and transformative leadership.
  • The AI Inflection Point & The Founder's Edge: Packed panels diving into artificial intelligence enterprise adoption and real-world startup journeys.
  • Guindy Angels Surge: Direct testament to the weekend's momentum: Guindy Angels registered 5 new signups within 48 hours!
  • Table Talks & Music Celebration: Deep conversations across cohorts followed by an electric musical celebration reliving college memories.

Keep the Momentum Going: Connect & Take Action

  1. Connect with CEGAANA on LinkedIn (< 1 Minute):
    • Path A — Follow the Official Page (5 Seconds): Visit linkedin.com/company/cegaana-org and click + Follow (and tap the 🔔 bell icon for "All posts") to get updates in your feed.
    • Path B — Link Degree Affiliation: On your LinkedIn profile (linkedin.com/in/), scroll to Education, click the pencil icon next to your CEG degree, search for "College of Engineering, Guindy", and select it from the dropdown so the official crest displays.
    • Path C — Add Volunteer Experience: If you serve as an organizer, chapter lead, or volunteer, add CEGAANA under your profile's Volunteering section.
    • Path D — Join the Discussion Group: Request to join the CEGAANA Official LinkedIn Group for peer-to-peer conversations, job postings, mentor connections, and batch networking.
  2. Join Local Chapters & WhatsApp Groups: Connect with local alumni chapters across the US and Canada via cegaana.org/connect and register your profile at members.cegaana.org. Things move much faster when your profile is linked!
  3. Support CEG Scholarships & Betterment: Donate Online via Stripe or Make a Pledge.
