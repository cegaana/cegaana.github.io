CEG Heritage & Impact

Saturday, September 26, 2026

First-Ever CEG Executives, Founders, and Investors Meetup

All of us have experienced the immense value from the "Red Bricks" College of Engineering, Guindy campus in shaping our education, careers, and life.

We are thrilled to bring alumni together for an in-person Connect, Engage, & Grow (CEG) event.

💡 Thanks to generous donations from alumni like you, CEGAANA has sponsored dozens of full scholarships for deserving CEG students over the past decade alongside several critical campus betterment projects.
